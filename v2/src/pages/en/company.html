About us

We are a small team of senior engineers. We design software and infrastructure, build it and keep it running for companies that need it to work, and to keep working.

Covering the whole cycle is a deliberate choice. The engineer who designed a system knows why it was built that way, and what we learn from running it goes straight back into the design.

We also publish the tools we build for our own work as open source, so anyone can read the code and judge its quality for themselves.

Our principle

“Because quality matters” is a principle our founder has worked by for a whole career. It applies to every part of IT: code, infrastructure, operations, documentation and the way we communicate.

Tested and reviewed
Changes ship after automated tests and a second pair of eyes, not before.
Open by default
We publish our tools and document what they do and what they don’t.
Built to recover
Systems are designed to fail safely and come back without heroics.
We question the plan
We ask awkward questions early. When a plan looks risky or wrong, we tell you directly, explain why and propose an alternative, because problems are cheapest to fix before anything is built.

Who we answer to

Our clients and our own standards, nobody else.

Bixority is owned by its founder. It has no investors, takes no subsidies or grants, has no loans and belongs to no group. No funding round, grant condition or investor’s exit plan decides what we build, how fast we grow or what we value.
