Data processed when you visit

To deliver a page, our hosting provider, Amazon Web Services, receives the technical data your browser sends with every request: your IP address, the address you asked for, the time, and request headers such as your browser type and the page that linked to ours.

We use these data only to deliver the website and to keep it secure. Where they are written to logs, we keep them only as long as that purpose needs and then delete them. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and your message to reply. If the conversation leads to a contract, we keep the correspondence for as long as the law requires, for example for accounting. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, where you ask about working together, steps taken before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Who else receives the data

Amazon Web Services hosts the website and processes the request data on our behalf under its data processing terms. To deliver pages quickly, it may do so in locations outside the European Economic Area; those transfers are covered by the standard contractual clauses in its terms. Email is handled by our email service provider. We do not sell or share personal data for any other purpose.
